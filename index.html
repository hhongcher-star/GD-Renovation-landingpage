About Us

"Some people look for a beautiful place, we make a place beautiful!"

GD Renovation is a interior design and decorator company which established in 2014, with a team of rich experience and industry knowledge for commercial and individual homes. We as a team will always prioritize customers needs and wants besides pursuing perfection and providing first-Class services to our valued customers.
